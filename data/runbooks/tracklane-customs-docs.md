# Regenerate customs documents in Tracklane

Owner: Operations systems. Class: LOL Internal. Last reviewed: 2026-06-05.

1. Open the booking in Tracklane → Documents.
2. Commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin regenerate from booking data. Fix the booking first (consignee, HS codes, country of origin), then Regenerate.
3. Signed documents from the shipper (for example BUM's signed packing list) are uploaded, not generated. They arrive by email to customs-docs@ and must be attached to the booking by the ops coordinator.
4. For India, invoices need the consignee's GST number. For EU exports from July 2026, check the HS codes against the new list.
5. Send regenerated documents to the broker from Tracklane, not from personal email, so there's a record.

If the broker portal rejects the file format, raise to Operations systems with the rejection message.
