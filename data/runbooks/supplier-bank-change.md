# Supplier asks to change bank details

Owner: AP controls. Class: LOL Confidential. Last reviewed: 2026-06-24.

This is the most common way companies get defrauded. Treat every request as suspicious until verified.

1. Never act on an email, however real it looks. Check the sender's domain against the supplier record; a free email domain is a stop.
2. Call the supplier on the phone number already in the supplier record, not the one in the email. Confirm the change with someone you can name.
3. Two people in AP controls approve the change in InvoiceHub. The requester can't be one of them.
4. Hold the first payment to the new account for 5 working days and confirm receipt with the supplier.
5. If you suspect fraud, forward the email to security and don't reply to it.

A supplier existing twice with different bank details is an incident, not a data-quality issue.
