# Visitor passes and badges

Owner: Security (Armadillo Security Services on site). Class: LOL Internal. Last reviewed: 2026-04-30.

1. Visitor passes: the host requests at least one working day ahead with names, company and date. Armadillo prints them at reception.
2. New employee badges need HR's confirmation and, for warehouse and driver roles, a cleared background check from Armadillo.
3. Background check results are ASS Restricted. Don't paste them into tickets, emails or any AI tool. Say "cleared" or "not cleared" only.
4. A badge that stops working: check it isn't expired in the badge system, then re-encode at reception.
5. Temporary badges for agency staff expire after 30 days and need a photo.
