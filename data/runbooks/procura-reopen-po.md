# Reopen a closed purchase order in Procura

Owner: Procurement systems. Class: LOL Internal. Last reviewed: 2026-04-12.

POs close automatically 30 days after the last receipt, or when the invoiced amount reaches the PO value.

1. Check why it closed. In Procura, open the PO, History tab. "Auto-close: receipt age" means it timed out; "Fully invoiced" means the money is used up.
2. If it timed out and the supplier still has a valid invoice, reopen it: Actions → Reopen → reason "Late invoice". Needs the buyer role.
3. If it's fully invoiced, don't reopen. Raise a PO change for more value; that needs the budget owner's approval.
4. Tell the requester the PO number and that the receipt can now be posted.

Don't reopen POs for suppliers on hold (red flag on the supplier record). Send those to Procurement.
